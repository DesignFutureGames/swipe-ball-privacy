2. Information Collected and Processed

The Developer does not operate its own account registration system or independent user database, and as a general rule does not permanently store users' personal information on its own servers. However, the following information may be processed while users use the Service.

  • Locally stored device data: Game progress, settings, temporary data, and other data stored locally on the user's device.
  • When using Google Play Games: Player identification information, sign-in status, achievements, leaderboard records, and information related to gameplay.
  • When using Google Play Billing: Transaction-related information required to verify purchase status, purchased products, and purchase restoration.
  • When using Google AdMob: Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, user interaction information, diagnostic information, and similar data may be processed for advertising, ad performance measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • When using Firebase Analytics: App usage data, event records, usage patterns, device and application environment information, and identifiers used for analytics.
  • When using Firebase Crashlytics: App crash information, error logs, device status at the time of a crash, operating system information, app version, and diagnostic information used to analyze service stability.

Some or all of this information may be processed automatically through external services or SDKs such as Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics rather than being directly entered by users and collected by the Developer.

3. Purposes of Processing Information

The Developer may process information to the extent necessary for the provision and operation of the Service for the following purposes.

  • Providing core game functions, including game operation, saving settings, and maintaining game progress.
  • Providing Google Play Games sign-in, achievements, and global leaderboard features.
  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing and verifying purchase restoration.
  • Providing advertisements, measuring advertising performance, and preventing advertising-related abuse or fraud.
  • Analyzing usage patterns, improving game content and features, and optimizing Service operation.
  • Analyzing and responding to app crashes, errors, unexpected termination, and performance issues.
  • Detecting and preventing abnormal use, misuse, abuse, or fraudulent activity.

4. Retention Period

Because the Developer does not operate its own server-based membership database, the Service is generally not structured to store users' personal information independently for extended periods.

  • Locally stored device data: May be deleted when the application is uninstalled, app data is reset, the device is changed, or the user manually deletes the data.
  • Google Play Games / Google Play Billing / Google AdMob / Firebase-related information: Retention periods may vary depending on the policies and system structure of each service provider, the user's account status, and applicable laws.
  • Support emails: Communications may be retained for a reasonable period as necessary for customer support, handling inquiries, and resolving disputes.
